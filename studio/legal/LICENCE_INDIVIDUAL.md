# Individual licence — Global Research Dataset / Academic Studio

**Version:** 0.2 (draft for product counsel review — not legal advice)

## Grant

Subject to a single purchase payment and these terms, you receive a non-exclusive,
non-transferable, perpetual individual licence. The licence is **included in the
purchase price** — there is no separate activation fee.

Sold editions:

- **Global Research Dataset** (`standard_dataset`) — full catalog as
  machine-readable exports (Excel/CSV/JSON/SQLite) personalized to your delivery.
  No device binding.
- **Academic Studio** (`academic_studio`) — complete research application software
  plus the full catalog (encrypted on-device), authorized on up to **two** of your
  devices, with device replacement and recovery.

Encrypted packaging used inside Academic Studio is part of that product. It is
not a separately sold “protected” tier.

## Restrictions

- Do not share activation credentials, licence files, or unlocked catalogs.
- Do not redistribute CSV/Excel/JSON/SQLite exports, database files, or dumps.
- Do not attempt to circumvent device binding, encryption, or entitlement checks.
- Licence sharing, unauthorized resale, and unauthorized redistribution are prohibited.
- Multi-user or institutional redistribution requires a future multi-user arrangement
  (not offered in this individual licence).

## Attribution of leaks

Personalized export identifiers and delivery manifests may identify a delivery if
a copy appears publicly. A fingerprint match is not automatic proof that you
pirated the product — theft from your account or device is possible. We review
evidence before enforcement.

## Updates and support

Perpetual usage does **not** mean unlimited future updates, indefinite support,
or third-party AI credits. See `studio/legal/UPDATES_AND_SUPPORT.md`.

Legitimate customers may receive dataset corrections and software updates for
the purchased version under the launch-documented window while their entitlement
remains valid. Pirated or revoked copies are not supported.

## Refunds and revocation

If a refund is issued after delivery of downloadable files, the entitlement may be
revoked and will fail at the next licence validation for Academic Studio. Dataset
file refunds are contractual for already-downloaded copies.

## No warranty of absolute copy-proofing

Technical protections raise the cost of casual redistribution. They do not make
extraction after authorized decryption impossible.
