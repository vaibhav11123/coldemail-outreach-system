# Individual licence — Global Research Faculty Dataset / Academic Studio

**Version:** 0.1 (draft for product counsel review — not legal advice)

## Grant

Subject to payment and these terms, you receive a non-exclusive, non-transferable
licence for personal academic use of the purchased edition:

- **Standard Dataset** — machine-readable exports personalized to your delivery.
- **Protected Dataset** — encrypted catalog unlocked on up to two of your devices.
- **Academic Studio** — Protected Dataset plus the research application features.

You may use the full faculty catalog offline after activation (Protected / Studio).

## Restrictions

- Do not share activation credentials, licence files, or unlocked catalogs.
- Do not redistribute CSV/Excel/JSON exports, database files, or derived dumps.
- Do not attempt to circumvent device binding, encryption, or entitlement checks.
- Commercial resale, shared lab licences, or institutional redistribution require
  a commercial licence.

## Attribution of leaks

Personalized export identifiers and delivery manifests may identify a delivery if
a copy appears publicly. A fingerprint match is not automatic proof that you
pirated the product — theft from your account or device is possible. We review
evidence before enforcement.

## Updates and support

Legitimate customers may receive dataset corrections and software updates while
their entitlement remains valid. Pirated or revoked copies are not supported.

## Refunds and revocation

If a refund is issued after delivery, the entitlement may be revoked and will
fail at the next licence validation.

## No warranty of absolute copy-proofing

Technical protections raise the cost of casual redistribution. They do not make
extraction after authorized decryption impossible.
