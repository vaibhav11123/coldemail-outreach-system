# Updates, support, and third-party AI — policy draft

**Version:** 0.1 (draft for counsel — not legal advice)

This document separates **perpetual licence** from **updates**, **support**, and **third-party AI**. Launch marketing must not blur these.

## Perpetual licence (included in purchase)

| Product | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Global Research Dataset | Right to use the purchased dataset version forever for the licensed individual, subject to licence terms. |
| Academic Studio | Right to use the purchased software version forever on up to two authorized devices, subject to licence terms. |

## Updates (not unlimited by default)

- Dataset corrections for the purchased version may be offered.
- Software updates for the purchased major version may be offered for a **documented window** set before commercial launch.
- Perpetual licence does **not** automatically include every future major version or indefinite catalog refreshes.
- Exact update duration is a commercial decision still to be finalized (provisional prices $14.99 / $39.99 do not yet encode an update SLA).

## Support (not indefinite by default)

- Support for installation, activation, and documented workflows may be included for a launch-defined period.
- Pirated, cracked, or revoked copies are not supported.
- Extended support may be offered later without creating a mandatory “commercial licence surcharge” on the base individual SKUs.

## Third-party AI

- Academic Studio uses **customer-connected** AI providers (BYOAI / local models).
- The purchase price does **not** include OpenAI, Anthropic, Groq, or other vendor API credits.
- Offline deterministic drafting remains available without AI.

## Refunds and already-downloaded files

- After a refund or chargeback, Academic Studio entitlements are revoked and fail at next validation where technically enforceable.
- Raw Dataset files already downloaded cannot be remotely erased; policy must state this limitation honestly.
