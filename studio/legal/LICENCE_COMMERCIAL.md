# Commercial / institutional licence — draft

**Version:** 0.1 (for counsel review)

Covers labs, consultancies, and organizations that need multi-seat access or
redistribution of derived work product (not the raw catalog) under contract.

Contact sales for seat counts, update SLAs, and audit terms. Device limits,
delivery fingerprinting, and entitlement revocation still apply unless the
executed order form states otherwise.

Unauthorized public posting of the catalog remains prohibited. Suspected leaks
are investigated against the delivery ledger before customer contact.
