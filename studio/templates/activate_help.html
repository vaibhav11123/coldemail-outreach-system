Installation & licence recovery

Academic Studio is sold as a one-time purchase. The licence is included. This page covers common failure cases without requiring paid support tools.

Typical journey

  1. Pay through the approved checkout or payment link.
  2. Receive a private download within the stated delivery window (usually within 24 hours after payment clears).
  3. Install or open Academic Studio.
  4. Open Activate, enter your licence id, and either activate on this device or send an offline activation request.
  5. Start researching offline. Connect AI or email later if you want.

If something goes wrong

  • Paid but received nothing — Email support with the payment reference. We verify the provider record and resend.
  • Download interrupted / ZIP corrupt — Ask for a fresh link. Checksums are in manifest.json.
  • Licence rejected — Read the exact error on Activate (wrong device, revoked, issuer mismatch, expired entitlement).
  • New computer — Use Activate with “replace previous device id” (two devices maximum).
  • AI or email connection fails — Built-in drafts and .eml export still work offline.
  • Workspace damaged — Use Backup / restore. Re-download the purchased catalog package if needed.
  • Refund — Studio entitlements are revoked after refund; already-downloaded dataset files are contractual.

Public vs private files

The Studio installer (when published) may be shared as a downloadable program. Your personalized dataset, CustomerLicense.aslic, and private download links must not be forwarded. Forwarding the installer alone must not unlock another person’s catalog.
